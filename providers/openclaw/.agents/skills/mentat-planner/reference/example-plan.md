# One plan, in the shape this skill produces

A drinks retailer that has traded for years and wants to be first online in its country. The project is at tier T8; every operation is open. This is the shape; the words are the person's.

**Mission** `G-01` — Become the first online drinks store in the country. Counted in online revenue (EUR per month), target 2,000,000 at least, by 2027-12-31. Latest: none.

**Thesis** (round 2, `set_roadmap_thesis`): "We win on revenue by December 2027 if: the customers who already buy from us buy more often and the ones who left come back (Customer Segments, Customer Relationships); our range and our prices beat the leader on the bottles that matter (Value Proposition, Revenue Streams); every visit is tracked and every channel is measured before money goes into it (Channels); the cost of a delivered bottle is known to the cent (Cost Structure); and we buy better than the market because we know the supply calendar (Key Partners, Market Mechanics)."

**Phases** (round 3, one `create_goal` each under `G-01`):

| Phase | Blocks | Starts | Deadline | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `G-02` Know ourselves | CS, RS, CO, CH, CR, KR, KA, KP | now | 2026-10-31 | Customer Segments and Revenue Streams filled from the order history; every visitor tracked; no "unknown" left on Key Resources |
| `G-03` Know the market | CL, MM, ID | 2026-10-01 | 2026-11-15 | competitors' prices read daily; the supply calendar has dates, minimums and money |
| `G-04` Decide the model | every block | 2026-11-15 | 2026-11-30 | a decision recorded per block |
| `G-05` Build the model | VP, CH, CR, RS, KA, KP | 2026-12-01 | 2027-06-30 | the decided moves shipped |
| `G-06` Scale | every block | 2027-07-01 | 2027-12-31 | monthly revenue read against the leader's |

**Rows of `G-02`** (round 4): `customer-jobs-pains-gains` placed 1, `cost-structure` placed 2, `channel-selection` placed 3 — each `link_to_goal` to `G-02`; `persona` skipped: "we sell to thousands of buyers we already know by their orders, not to a persona"; the idea `IDEA-12` "reopen the tax warehouse" adopted and placed as a move in `G-05`; gaps: `W-01` "Export the order history since 2009 into the analysis store", `W-02` "Enter purchase prices per product" — both `Proposed`, about `G-02`.

**Measures** (round 5): `G-02` measures "monthly online revenue, website against shop, from the order history"; `G-05` measures "conversion per channel and repeat rate".

**Decision** `DEC-01` — "Plan v1 approved — roadmap version 14", `Proposed` until the person accepts it on the decisions tab.
