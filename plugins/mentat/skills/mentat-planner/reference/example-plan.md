# One plan, in the shape this skill produces

A drinks retailer that has traded for years and wants to be first online in its country. The project stands at T2; every operation is open. This is the shape; the words are the person's.

**Mission** `G-01` (round 1) — the ambition: Become the first online drinks store in the country. Counted in online revenue (EUR per month), target 2,000,000 at least, by 2027-12-31. Latest: none.

**Objectives** (round 1, one `create_goal` each under `G-01`) — outcomes in the market, never work done:

| Objective | Counted in | Target | By |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `G-02` Customers who buy online again within 60 days | repeat customers | 4,000 at least | 2027-03-31 |
| `G-03` Monthly online revenue from the beachhead | EUR per month | 300,000 at least | 2027-06-30 |
| `G-04` Customers who order through a referral | customers per month | 500 at least | 2027-09-30 |

The person asked for "20 supplier calls held" as an objective; it was left to the mapping operation as the pass line of an experiment. They asked for "Value Proposition confidence 0.7"; the gates check that before the build, so no objective was written.

**Thesis** (round 2, `set_roadmap_thesis`): "We win on revenue by December 2027 if: the customers who already buy from us buy more often and the ones who left come back (Customer Segments, Customer Relationships); our range and our prices beat the leader on the bottles that matter (Value Proposition, Revenue Streams); every visit is tracked and every channel is measured before money goes into it (Channels); the cost of a delivered bottle is known to the cent (Cost Structure); and we buy better than the market because we know the supply calendar (Key Partners, Market Mechanics)."

**Tiers** (round 3, `set_roadmap_tier_date`): T2 by 2026-12-31; T3 by 2027-02-28; T4 by 2027-04-30; T5 and T6 no date.

**Rows** (round 4): in T2, `customer-profile` placed first; `persona` skipped: "we sell to thousands of buyers we already know by their orders, not to a persona"; the idea `IDEA-12` "reopen the tax warehouse" adopted and placed as a move in T5. Gaps: "no purchase prices per product" — a note on the `what-they-use-today` row; "the monthly running costs" — `W-01` "Enter monthly running costs", a task under the `cost-structure` row, for the founder, in To do; "talk to 10 restaurant buyers" — a note on the `hypotheses-mapping-and-experiments-design` row, as a test. No row moved for a gate.

**Decision** `DEC-01` — "Plan v1 approved — roadmap version 14", `Proposed` until the person accepts it on the decisions tab.
